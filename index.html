How it works

Set the phone down. Play your game.

01 · Follow

It follows the ball

From any tripod. An optional €20 pod adds a motor that pans with the play.

02 · Track

It knows every player

A box on every player, jersey numbers read, positions mapped to the court.

03 · Score

It keeps the score

Shots are called and box scores fill in as you play. Any call can be fixed with one tap.

04 · Stream

It goes live

A clean picture with a score overlay on YouTube, for family anywhere in the world.

Questions

Good to know

Do I need the pod?

No. HooperGPT works from any tripod or a fixed spot at the side of the court. The pod is an optional 3D-printed head with a small motor that turns the phone to follow the play.

Where does the video go?

The model runs on the phone. Games stay in a private team archive, and they're only streamed when the team chooses to go live.

What about filming minors?

Youth games need consent from a parent or guardian under GDPR. HooperGPT includes a consent roster, and we start with adult leagues and teams that opt in.

How accurate is it?

We publish our numbers above, including the weak ones. Every automatic call can be corrected with one tap, and corrections make the model better.
